FAQ

Questions Before You Start

How much does a website cost in Bengaluru?

It depends on the number of pages, the features and how much custom design and development you need. A brochure site and an online store with payments are very different builds. Send us your requirements and we will reply with a clear quote and a list of what it includes.

How long does it take to build a website?

Smaller business websites usually take a few weeks. Online stores and custom web applications take longer. We agree on a timeline with you before work starts, and it depends partly on how quickly content and feedback arrive.

Should I choose WordPress or a custom website?

Choose WordPress if your team wants to edit pages and publish content regularly without a developer. Choose a custom build if you need unusual features, complex logic or maximum performance. We build both, so we recommend whichever suits your project.

Will my website rank on Google?

We build every site with the technical foundations search engines look for, including clean HTML, fast loading, mobile-friendly layouts and structured data. Nobody can honestly guarantee a specific ranking, because results also depend on your content, your competition and your reputation online.

Should I use Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google Ads reach people who are already searching for what you offer, so they often suit services and urgent needs. Meta Ads on Instagram and Facebook reach people by interests, location and behaviour before they start searching, so they often suit visual products, offers and building awareness. Many businesses use both. We recommend a mix based on your goals and budget.

Is the ad budget included in your fee?

No. Your ad budget is paid directly to Google or Meta and is separate from our fee for planning and managing the campaigns. We agree on both amounts with you before anything goes live.

Can you guarantee leads or sales from ads?

No one can honestly guarantee that, because results depend on your offer, your market, your budget and your competition. We set up conversion tracking, test what works, and show you what your budget produced so you can make clear decisions.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We review what works on your current site, rebuild what does not, and set up redirects from old addresses so you keep as much of your existing search traffic as possible.

Do you only work with businesses in Bengaluru?

Our studio is in Nayanda Halli, Bengaluru, and we are happy to meet local clients. We also work with businesses elsewhere over WhatsApp, email and video calls.

Do you work with businesses in my industry?

Most likely. We build for service businesses, retailers, clinics, schools, restaurants, startups and many other fields. Tell us what your customers need to do on your website and we will plan the build around that.

Can you maintain my website after launch?

Yes. We can handle updates, backups, security checks and content changes after launch, so you can keep your attention on your business.

Do I own the website when it is finished?

Yes. Once the project is complete and paid for, your website, content and domain belong to you.
